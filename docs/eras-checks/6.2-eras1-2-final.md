# Step 6.2: the last Eras 1-2 races (south-africa-1983, france-1990, monaco-1992, brazil-1993)

Eras build plan step 6.2, Nov 8 2026. These four races complete Eras 1 and 2. All four are
reconstructed, Eras-only races (in no Daily or Gauntlet pool), so their overlays are built into
their race files. Every lap is synthetic (docs/eras-checks/r2-reconstruction-facts.md), and every
briefing says that lap times are reconstructed while lap leaders are sourced. Nothing below
presents a synthetic gap or lap as fact.

Where the content lives:
- `data/eras/<id>.json`: the `eras` block (target, storyline, mastery, hidden, briefing, `eraId`,
  `order`, `hardCalls` / `userSide`, monaco-1992's `storylineCalls`) and `storylineEvents`.
- `data/moments/<id>.json`: the decision moments (pit-board labels), built into the race file.
- `pipeline/src/pipeline/build.py`: monaco-1992's registry side, flipped to Senna.

Check each fact against its link, then put your initials and the date in **Owner checked**.

**south-africa-1983 does not follow the count: owner decision needed.** The 0-0 tie goes to
Prost by 0.3 points. But Prost retired on lap 35 with turbo failure (WP-RSA83, F1DB "Turbo",
35 laps; MS-RSA83: "turbo-charger trouble", no lap; HRP-RSA83 says lap 33). His retirement is in
the race file (RETIREMENT, lap 36), so from Prost's side every path retires 11 laps after the
takeover (lap 25). An avoidable storyline retirement needs the real call that caused it (the owner
rule: the car retires only if the player repeats the real call), and no source read ties the
turbo failure to a call: WP, MS, GPE and HRP give no boost or pace setting for Prost before it
(HRP gives no boost setting for Prost either). Inventing one would break the no-invented-facts rule. So
the race stays on Piquet, whose side is the race's storyline (the spec's draft target "Piquet
high enough for the title"). Note: re-running `pipeline hard-calls south-africa-1983` writes
`userSide: PRO` again; set it back (or the owner approves Prost with a sourced condition).
