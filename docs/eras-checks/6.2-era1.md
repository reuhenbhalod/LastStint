# Step 6.2: Era 1 content (1984-1988)

Eras build plan step 6.2, Nov 8 2026. Covers the nine Era 1 races after south-africa-1983:
monaco-1984, portugal-1984, germany-1986, portugal-1985, mexico-1986, hungary-1986,
australia-1986, great-britain-1987 and monaco-1988. All nine are Eras-only (in no Daily or
Gauntlet pool), so their overlays are built into their race files. All nine are reconstructed
races: every lap is synthetic (docs/eras-checks/r2-reconstruction-facts.md), and every briefing
says so. Nothing below presents a synthetic gap or lap as fact.

Where the content lives:
- `data/eras/<id>.json`: the `eras` block (target, storyline, mastery, hidden, briefing; plus
  `eraId`, `order`, and `hardCalls` / `userSide` from `pipeline hard-calls`) and
  `storylineEvents`.
- `data/moments/<id>.json`: the decision moments, built into the race file by `pipeline build`.
- `pipeline/src/pipeline/build.py`: great-britain-1987's registry side, flipped to Piquet.

Check each fact against its link, then put your initials and the date in **Owner checked**.

## Sim limits found (for the owner)

1. **The grader leaves the fuel load out** (grade.ts: "The grader's playouts leave the fuel load
   out"). In a fuel-limit race running dry is invisible to the grades: PUSH grades best and lift
   and coast grades worse, though in play push runs the car dry and lifting is what reaches the
   flag. So the late fuel calls offer hold / lift / full mixture and no PUSH to the flag (a
   graded PUSH would reward running dry), and great-britain-1987's pace calls offer no PUSH.
   Fuel calls are graded only on their lap-time cost.
2. **The takeover fuel margin is drawn around zero** (−1.5 to +1.0 kg). On the checker's seeds
   1-3 the player runs dry on the last lap with no calls in germany-1986, mexico-1986,
   great-britain-1987 and monaco-1988, and finishes in the other five. Whether doing nothing
   finishes is down to the seed, not the race.
3. **monaco-1984's fuel burn is the allowance over 31 laps** (the results lap), about 6 kg a lap,
   so in the sim Prost can run dry on lap 31 (5 of seeds 1-8 with no calls). The real race was
   stopped short in the rain, so fuel wasn't the real problem; the briefing makes no fuel claim
   beyond the allowance. Known as item 16 in docs/eras-races-report.md.
4. **Frozen storyline retirements** (germany-1986, australia-1986, monaco-1988): the checker
   fails on all three until the avoidable-retirement work lands; CI's reachability step will
   fail on them too. Their content passes with the retirement taken out.
5. **Flat grades.** With the reconstructed gaps, most calls grade within a few points of each
   other, so the mastery objectives carry the challenge. The checker's beam (4) breaks ties by
   option order, so where the mastery call is graded level with staying out it comes first in the
   race (portugal-1984's fuel call, hungary-1986's and great-britain-1987's stop at 34-35), and
   portugal-1985's fresh-wets option is listed first.

## Doubts (synthetic details that don't match the sources)

- great-britain-1987: at the takeover (lap 32) the reconstruction has Mansell 10.5 s ahead of
  Piquet; WP has Mansell about 2 s behind at lap 35. Hence `win`, not `defend`.
- australia-1986: at the takeover (lap 29) the reconstruction has Streiff leading and Rosberg
  sixth, 62 s behind. Streiff finished fifth (Jolpica), and no source read gives the lap-29
  order, so this isn't confirmed wrong, but it looks wrong. Worth a solver look.
- hungary-1986: Streiff is third at the takeover, 71 s behind but ahead of Mansell.
- monaco-1988: Senna leads Prost by 104 s at the takeover (lap 36); WP has about 50 s at lap 54.
