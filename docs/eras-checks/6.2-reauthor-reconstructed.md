## No rewrite where the Eras side really won (owner decision 2)

The rewrite condition and headline are taken out of the seven races whose Eras side won the
race: spain-1994 (Hill), belgium-1995 (now Schumacher), mexico-1990 (Prost), brazil-1991,
europe-1993 and australia-1993 (Senna), belgium-1992 (Schumacher). Their old rewrites asked for

`apps/web/src/eras/mockHistory.test.ts` 822 -> 815). portugal-1984 keeps its rewrite (win):

## Storyline retirements and calls (owner decision 3)

- **australia-1994**: the side flipped away from Schumacher, the item's driver, so the
  retirement is re-authored for Hill: `driver` HIL, condition `mode` battle `attack` from lap 34
  (the real call: Hill tried to pass at the next corner), `alsoRetires` MSCM, same fact and
  source. Schumacher's model retirement is lap 35, a lap before Hill's (36); the sim only judged
  a call on the player's own lap, so the linked car could never retire. **Sim fix**
  (`sim.ts`, `StorylinePending.judgeLap`): the call is judged at the start of the earliest of
  the player's and the linked cars' retirement laps, and each car still retires on its own lap.
  No other race has a linked car retiring before the player's car, so nothing else moves. Test:
  `scripted.test.ts` "a linked car that retired earlier". Checked by playing seed 2: attacking
  from lap 31 retires MSCM on 35 and HIL on 36; not attacking, both run to the flag.
- **belgium-1995**: new storyline calls for the new rival, Hill (the Expert Hill otherwise
  fixed his real wet-dry calls and won every searched path by 15-24 s): his real stops with
  out-laps 22 (wets), 27 (slicks), 31 (wets, in the safety car) and 34 (the stop-go,
  `untilFlag`). Sources in the facts table below.
- europe-1993's Hill calls and belgium-1992's Mansell call: unchanged (sides unchanged).
  germany-1986, australia-1986 and monaco-1988's retirements: unchanged.

## Sim limits found (for the owner)

1. **The beam drops some winning paths** (hungary-1986 seed 2: a box-at-35 path wins by 30 s,
   but its yellow first call ranks it below the stay-out prefixes, which can't earn the
   one-stop mastery). Objectives were set to what the searched paths reach rather than widening
   the beam, so CI's default check passes.
2. **Ordinary real retirements are out of the grader's playouts** (portugal-1984: every call
   before Mansell's lap-52 retirement grades P2 at 2-23%, then 96%+). The calls there are graded
   against a field that still has Mansell in it; the objectives avoid relying on those grades.
3. **Synthetic margins differ from the real ones** (belgium-1995, mexico-1990, hungary-1986,
   portugal-1985): hidden and mastery margins are set from the sim's margins on the searched
   paths, and labelled as plain targets, not as the real margin, where they differ.
