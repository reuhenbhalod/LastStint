**austria-2002 takeover pinned to lap 26** (was the D3 rule's lap 21). From lap 21 the first call
is the stop under the lap-24 safety car, and no option a moment can offer matches what the grader
prices: its continuation refuels to the flag with no fill cap, so "stay out" always grades best,
and a car that stays out runs dry on lap 27. No path could earn ★3. From lap 26 both Ferraris
have made that stop (their real in-lap 24, RAW). From lap 21 the counter gave MSCM 0, BAR 0 (tie
to MSCM by chance given up); from lap 26 it gives BAR 1, MSCM 0, so the side is Barrichello, the
spec's driver. A sim fix (a fill to the flag on the pit card, or a capped continuation) would let
the lap-24 call come back. That's a question for the owner, not done here.

## How the moments were authored

The recipe's option sets (packages/sim/scripts/authoring.ts: stay / box per compound / box next
lap; push / standard / manage / push and attack; defend / hold / attack). Each moment is graded
against the race's own Eras target (`graderTarget`), not beat-rival. abu-dhabi-2016 and
spain-2013 keep the `pnpm author` picks (retargeted). For the other four the laps were chosen by
hand: the checker plays moment answers only, and the player's car never stops between moments,
so a race needs a stop call on the lap the grader's planner would stop (`planRemainingStop` along
the path). Without one, every "stay out" grades best and the car runs its first set to the flag.
austria-2002's stop calls also offer the short and long fills (the pit card's three choices).
